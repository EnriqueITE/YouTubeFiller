YouTube
Filler.

Edge to edge, with your tabs in reach.

Expand the video to fill the tab. The address bar and browser controls stay on screen.

Chrome Web Store ↗Source code ↗

One button beside fullscreen.

Open a regular YouTube video and click the button with the arrows. The video keeps its proportions and crops the edges around the center to fill your tab. Click again to restore the page.

Works on youtube.com/watch. Shorts and embedded players are outside its scope.

Keyboard controls

º
Toggle fill mode on the video. Choose a different key in the extension menu.
Alt + Shift + V
Default browser command.
Esc
Leave fill mode.
